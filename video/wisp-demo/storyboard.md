# WISP demo film: storyboard (v2, ad-style)

**Format:** 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 300.0 s (9,000 frames) · H.264 + AAC · nine scenes, about 45 shots.
**Angle (Brag creative direction, re-tuned for an ad):** one human sentence, *"I feel weak,"* followed to a clear and honest next step. Short shots of 2–5 s, kinetic type that lands on the narration, springy UI, brand transitions and a 96 BPM score. The narration keeps a steady product-documentary tone.
**Style:** illustrated 2D with 2.5D camera moves, drawn natively in SVG inside Remotion. The palette is ivory, forest, sage, amber, and red for emergencies only. Headlines are set in Lora, UI in Inter. The WISP Line runs through every scene and transition.
**Real vs illustrated:** every phone screen and the Technical view are real captures of the running app (`capture/capture.spec.ts`). People, rooms and the sensing scenes are illustrations. WISP Sense is a labelled design concept.
**Tools:** Remotion is the master timeline. **HyperFrames** builds three motion-graphic inserts (the ident, the safety principle and the end card), which Remotion composites. ElevenLabs provides the narration and some of the SFX.

Shot times are absolute. `→` gives the transition into the next shot.

## Sound

- **Narration:** 15 ElevenLabs clips (`voiceover-script.md`); `wisp-demo.srt` is a sidecar subtitle file.
- **Score:** original, composed in code (`scripts/make_music.py`), at 96 BPM so each bar is 2.5 s and every scene boundary lands on a bar line. It has a soft kick, snaps, shaker, bass, plucked arpeggios and a pad. The emergency section (2:45–3:07) has no drums. It builds through the roadmap and resolves at the logo.
- **Mix:** between lines the music sits about 3 dB under the voice; under narration it ducks about 7 dB further. Final loudness is −16 LUFS with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling.
- **SFX:** soft whooshes on cuts, pops on UI beats, a chime when screening completes, a settle tone for the recommendation, a restrained low tone for emergencies (never an alarm), and the ElevenLabs logo and sensing accents.
